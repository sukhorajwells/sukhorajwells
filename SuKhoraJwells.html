Our Story

Founded in April 2025, SuKhora was born from a passion for creating jewellery that redefines love and elegance. Our name, featuring the distinctive capital 'S' and 'K' followed by the symbolic pink lotus, represents purity, beauty, and royal elegance.

We specialize in artificial gold, silver, and platinum-plated jewellery that combines traditional craftsmanship with contemporary designs. Each piece is meticulously crafted to ensure exceptional quality and lasting beauty.

Our commitment is to provide affordable luxury without compromising on style or quality. From daily wear to special occasions, SuKhora offers something for every moment and every personality.
